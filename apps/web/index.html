Questions (answered)

(00.03)

Everything you need to know before your first session. Anything else, ask us at the end of a round.

(01)What kinds of interviews can I practise?

Technical, HR and Behavioral rounds. Each session follows a structured flow: introduction, interview, feedback and summary.

(02)Do I type my answers or say them?

PrepTalk is voice-first. You answer out loud, the way you would in a real interview, and you can mute or turn your video off at any time.

(03)What’s in my report?

An overall score plus scores for communication, technical accuracy, confidence and clarity, along with your strengths, areas to improve and personalised suggestions.

(04)How long is a session?

Most rounds take 15–25 minutes. You can end early at any point and still get a report for the questions you answered.
